Six commitments

The two questions every buyer of a new premium brand asks: what will it cost to own, and who will look after it? Here are our answers.

Parts availability

Genuine JETOUR parts held in Kuwait, with a planned stock for the GAIA range, so your car is back on the road quickly.

Ownership cost

Service plans with clear prices, set out before you buy. No surprises at the service desk.

Warranty

Every GAIA sold by Al Mulla carries the manufacturer warranty, honoured in Kuwait. Your advisor walks you through the cover.

One number, day or night, anywhere in Kuwait: in the city or out in the desert.
